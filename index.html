What we do

Digital twins

Simulation and synchronised virtual models of physical assets, environments and systems. Usually the visualisation and simulation layer of a much larger programme.

Spatial computing

Immersive and 3D interfaces, real-time rendering, geospatial visualisation. Web, desktop or headset, depending on where the work needs to land.

Decision support

Turning complex, multi-source data into interfaces people can reason with: dashboards, maps, scenario tools.

How we work

Worldtick works as a specialist partner within larger delivery teams — bringing depth in the visualisation and simulation layer to programmes led by systems integrators and prime contractors.
